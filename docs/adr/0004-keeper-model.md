# ADR 0004 - Keeper Model

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Keeper-backed retention starts as managed and partner-operated, with no token economy.

## Why

The project needs sustainable retention without speculative governance or fake decentralization.

## Consequences

- managed replay can have real SLOs
- free tier remains local or best-effort
- third-party keeper enrollment comes only after attestation, compatibility policy, and operator metrics exist
